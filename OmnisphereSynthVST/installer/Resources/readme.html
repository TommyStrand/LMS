Getting Started

After installation, restart your DAW. SuperNovaPad will appear under TommyStrand in your instrument browser.

Texture Effects Chain

All effects are post-voice and run in this order:
Grit → Lo-Fi → Vinyl → Broken Tape Delay → Doubler → Distortion → Reverb → Shimmer

Uninstalling

Remove the following files/folders to uninstall:

  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/SuperNovaPad.vst3
  • /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/SuperNovaPad.component
  • /Applications/SuperNovaPad.app
