OmnisphereSynth 1.0.0

by TommyStrand

Welcome to OmnisphereSynth — a polyphonic synthesizer with multi-voice architecture, MIDI control, and a full texture effects chain.

What will be installed:

  • VST3 /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  • AU /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  • App /Applications/OmnisphereSynth.app

Voice engines: Subtractive Synth · Church Organ · Hammond B3 (Leslie) · Rhodes Mk1 (FM)

Texture effects: Lo-Fi · Vinyl Simulation · Broken Tape Delay · Grit (tube waveshaper) · Doubler

MIDI CC map (VST3/AU):
CC1 = Mod Wheel / LFO depth  ·  CC3 = Leslie speed  ·  CC11 = Expression  ·  CC74 = Filter cutoff

Requires macOS 12 or later · Apple Silicon and Intel supported
