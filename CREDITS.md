# Credits

## Samples

**Grand Piano** — *Salamander Grand Piano V3* by Alexander Holm, a Yamaha C5 grand.
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Source: <https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano>.
Changes made for this app: two of the sixteen velocity layers (v5, v12) for every
third semitone C1–C7, mixed to mono, resampled to 44.1 kHz / 16-bit, trimmed to
3–8 seconds with a fade-out, and peak-normalised. **The CC BY licence requires this
attribution; the app also shows it in Settings → About.**

**String Ensemble** and **Concert Flute** — *VSCO 2 Community Edition* by Versilian
Studios (recorded by Sam Gossner & Simon Dalzell), cello section, violin section and
solo flute. Released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
(public domain). Source: <https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE>.

All three are installed by `Scripts/fetch_free_samples.py`, which documents exactly
which files are used and how they are converted.

**Pipe Organ** (optional, not in this repository) — imported locally with
`Scripts/import_samples.py --source sfz`; check the licence of the library you import.
